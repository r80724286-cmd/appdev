# 🤖 My Favorite Movie: Transformers

## 🎬 About the Movie
**Transformers** is an action and science-fiction movie about giant robots that can transform into vehicles. The story focuses on the battle between two groups of robots: the Autobots and the Decepticons.

## 🚗 Main Characters
- **Optimus Prime** – The leader of the Autobots.
- **Bumblebee** – A brave and loyal Autobot.
- **Megatron** – The powerful leader of the Decepticons.
- **Sam Witwicky** – A human who becomes involved in the battle.

## ⭐ Why I Love This Movie
I love Transformers because:
1. It has amazing action scenes.
2. The robots have awesome transformations.
3. It teaches the importance of friendship and courage.
4. The special effects are impressive.
